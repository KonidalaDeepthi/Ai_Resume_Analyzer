About AI Resume Skill Matcher

AI Resume Skill Matcher is an innovative tool designed to help job seekers quickly assess their readiness for various roles in the tech industry. By analyzing your resume, our system extracts key skills and matches them against popular job profiles.

How it works:

  1. Upload your resume in PDF format or paste your resume text
  2. Our AI extracts technical and soft skills from your document
  3. We compare your skills against predefined job roles
  4. Get instant feedback on your match percentage and recommendations

Features:

  • Skill extraction from PDF resumes
  • Job role matching with detailed breakdowns
  • Resume strength scoring
  • Interview preparation questions
  • Learning recommendations for skill gaps

This tool is perfect for students, recent graduates, and professionals looking to pivot their careers. Know your job readiness in seconds!
